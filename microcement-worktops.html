Why Choose Microcement?

The Modern Alternative to Worktop Replacement

Microcement is a high-performance polymer-modified concrete coat hand-trowelled at just 2-3mm thickness. It bonds permanently to virtually any existing surface, creating a monolithic, joint-free concrete finish.

🛠️

No Removal Required

Avoid expensive plumbing, joinery, and tile demolition. We resurface directly over laminate, wood, granite, tile, and MDF counter tops.

💧

Seamless & Waterproof

Continuous trowelled finish with zero seams or grout lines where water, dirt, or bacteria can accumulate. 100% polyurethane sealed.

🎨

Bespoke Tonal Shades

Durable & Heat Resistant

Engineered for busy residential and commercial kitchens. Highly scratch-resistant, heat-resistant, and citrus/oil stain-proof.

How It Works

Our 4-Step Resurfacing Process

Transforming your kitchen worktop usually takes 3-4 days on site with minimal noise, dust, or kitchen downtime.

01

Surface Prep & Prime

We lightly abrade and degrease your existing worktops, applying a high-adhesion fiberglass-reinforced quartz primer.

02

Base Microcement Coat

First base coat of polymer-modified microcement is applied to reinforce edges, fill seams, and create a solid structural foundation.

03

Artisan Finish Trowel

Our craftsmen hand-trowel the fine finish layer, creating the bespoke visual texture, movement, and shade of natural concrete.

04

Polyurethane Sealing

We sand smoothly and apply 2-3 coats of heavy-duty matte or satin polyurethane sealant for 100% waterproof protection.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Microcement be applied over damaged laminate or timber?

Yes. Any minor chips or gouges in your existing worktop are filled and leveled during our preparation phase. As long as the existing substrate is securely fixed to your kitchen units, microcement will bond permanently.

Is Microcement easy to clean and stain-resistant?

Extremely easy. We apply high-grade polyurethane sealers that create a non-porous barrier. Daily cleaning requires just warm water and mild liquid soap. Oils, red wine, coffee, and lemon juice wipe straight off.

How long does the resurfacing process take?

An average kitchen worktop resurfacing project takes 3 to 4 days from preparation to final sealing. You can begin light usage of your kitchen within 24 hours after sealing.

How does microcement compare in cost to buying new granite or quartz?

Microcement typically costs 40% to 60% less than solid quartz or granite replacement because there are no expensive quarrying costs, no heavy demolition, and no plumbing altercations required.
