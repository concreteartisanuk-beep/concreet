How to Edit Your Site

Follow these steps every time you want to update your products, prices or text.


Editing Products & Prices

1

Open this Admin Panel

Go to concreet.co.uk/admin and log in with your password.

2

Make your changes

Click on the Shop or Furniture tab. Edit any product name, price or description directly in the text boxes.

3

Click "Save & Download"

Press the gold Save & Download button at the bottom. A file called site-data.js will download to your Downloads folder.

4

Replace the file on your PC

Move or copy the downloaded site-data.js into your site folder:
Echolift_SEO_SaaS → concreet-app → site-data.js
Replace the existing file when asked.

5

Deploy to Netlify

Go to app.netlify.com, open your concreet-shop-uk site, click Deploys, then drag your entire concreet-app folder onto the deploy zone. Done — your site is live within 30 seconds.

Changing a Product Photo

1

Name your new photo correctly

Rename your photo to match the existing image name, for example candle.jpg or clock-copper.webp. Check the product card in this admin panel to see the current filename.

2

Drop it into the images folder

Copy the photo to: concreet-app → images → and replace the old one.

3

Deploy as above

Drag the concreet-app folder to Netlify Deploys and your new photo goes live.

Changing Your Admin Password

1

Open admin/index.html

In your concreet-app folder, open admin → index.html in a text editor.

2

Find and change the password

Search for ADMIN_PASSWORD near the top of the script section and change it to your new password. Save and redeploy.
