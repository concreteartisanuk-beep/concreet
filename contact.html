Frequently asked

Can I order a custom size table?
Yes — all our furniture is made to order. Tell us your dimensions and we'll create something to fit your exact space.
How long does delivery take?
Small pieces (candles, soaps, bowls) ship within 5–7 days. Furniture is made to order — typically 3–5 weeks depending on complexity.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, just mention it in your order or message and we'll wrap it beautifully at no extra charge.
Are the products sealed or treated?
All homeware is sealed with a food-safe or water-resistant finish as appropriate. Tables and furniture receive a heavy-duty penetrating sealer for lasting durability.
